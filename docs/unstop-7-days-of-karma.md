## About the opportunity

> Small actions. Real impact. Recognition that matters.

**7 Days of Karma** is a free, seven-day online challenge from KarmaVerse by 3R ZeroWaste. Every day brings one small positive action you can do from your phone, your hostel or your home: a quick habit check, an eco quiz, a good-habit challenge, a friend invite, and a finale where you share your own green action.

Complete each day, submit your proof on KarmaVerse, collect campaign points and climb the live leaderboard. Finish at least 5 of the 7 days to earn a digital certificate with a verification QR code.

**How to take part**
1. Register here on Unstop.
2. Create your free KarmaVerse account and register for the challenge at **karmaverse.earth/events/7-days-of-karma**. Your daily tasks, points and certificate live there.
3. Each day from 28 Nov, open the day's task on KarmaVerse and submit your photo, link or answer.
4. Track your points and rank on the challenge leaderboard.

Missed a day? It stays open until 4 Dec, 11:59 PM IST. Earlier submissions win ties.

## Rewards and recognition

- **Digital certificate** for everyone who completes at least 5 of the 7 days. It carries a certificate ID and a QR code that anyone can verify.
- **Top participants featured** on KarmaVerse channels.
- **A place on the public leaderboard.**

## Rules

- One registration per person. Submit your own original work; copied or edited screenshots are rejected.
- Points count only after the KarmaVerse team approves a submission. If one is rejected, you'll see the reason and can submit again.
- Day 6 counts only when your friend registers for 7 Days of Karma with your KarmaVerse referral code.
- Ties on the leaderboard go to whoever reached the score first.
- The KarmaVerse team may remove entries or participants that break these rules. Its decisions on scoring are final.

## FAQs

**Is it free?**
Yes, completely.

**Do I need the KarmaVerse app?**
No. Everything works on karmaverse.earth in your phone's browser. The free account takes a minute to create.

**Why register on both Unstop and KarmaVerse?**
Unstop lists the challenge. Your daily tasks, points, leaderboard rank and certificate are on KarmaVerse.

**Can I join after 28 Nov?**
Yes, until registration closes on 1 Dec. Missed days stay open until the end.

**When do I get my certificate?**
After the challenge ends and all submissions are reviewed. Download it from My Events on KarmaVerse.

**Where is my referral code?**
In KarmaVerse under Referral. Your friend enters it when registering for 7 Days of Karma.
